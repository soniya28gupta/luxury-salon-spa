# Aura Sanctuary — Haute Coiffure, Luxury Spa & Wellness with AI Booking Agent

An ultra-luxurious, editorial wellness and salon platform featuring an autonomous conversational AI booking concierge and native Google Business Profile ("Reserve with Google") integration.

1. **Ultra-Luxurious Brand Aesthetic**:
   - Palette: Obsidian Noir (`#0a0a0c`), Champagne Gold (`#d4af37`), Silk Rose Bronze (`#b76e79`), and Velvet Cream (`#fbfbf8`).
   - Editorial serif typography (`Playfair Display`) paired with clean sans geometry (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
   - Ambient glow lighting, high-resolution imagery, and micro-interactions.

2. **Curated Treatment Collections**:
   - 24K Gold Cellular Renewal Facial (pure 24-karat gold leaf, ultrasound infusion, peptide collagen)

   - Royal Moroccan Hammam & Cedar Steam (heated marble slab, eucalyptus Savon Noir, Kessa exfoliation, rhassoul clay)
   - Chakra Balancing Warm Volcanic Stone Ritual (heated basalt stones, frankincense, lavender oils)
   - Sovereign Empress Penthouse Suite Experience (private VIP suite, Dom Pérignon champagne service, bridal couture trial)
   - Gentleman's Royal Barber & Executive Beard Sculpt

3. **Master Curators & Stylists**:
   - Laurent Mercier — Creative Hair Director (Paris / Milan trained)
   - Dr. Elena Rostova — Lead Dermal Biochemist & Aesthetician
   - Somsri Phan — Grand Master of Balinese & Thai Bodywork (Chiang Mai)
   - Ananya Deshmukh — Celebrity Makeup & Bridal Stylist (Vogue India featured)

4. **Autonomous AI Booking Concierge & Stylist Consultant**:
   - Natural language consultation: analyzes client concerns (e.g., fatigue, dry skin, balayage styling for gala events).
   - Tailored treatment recommendations with duration, pricing, and benefits.
   - Master Stylist pairing based on specialty.
   - Real-time time slot selector.

   - Official Google Maps listing card showing 4.98★ rating (842+ verified reviews), business hours, and address.

6. **Guest Sanctuary Portal**:
   - View confirmed upcoming appointments with unique passcodes.
   - 1-Click Reschedule or Cancel reservation.
   - Download `.ics` calendar invitation for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.
